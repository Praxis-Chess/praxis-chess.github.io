03Filter

Forty evaluations become the four that mattered, with the clock reading attached.

04Explain

08 / Install

Running in about
five minutes.

No account, nothing to sign in to. Start four local processes, give it your Chess.com username, and it does the rest.

Download Praxis
Does any of my data leave the machine? +

No. The only outbound request is to the public Chess.com archive endpoint, for games under your own username. No account, no sync service, no telemetry.

Do I need a GPU? +

No. A 7B model runs on CPU at a usable speed here, because it is asked for one short paragraph per mistake rather than a conversation.

Can I use a different engine or model? +

Any UCI engine works. The integration is the protocol, not the binary. Any model Ollama can serve works too.
